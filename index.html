Hello there! I'm Mary, and I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at Harvard advised by Professor Cengiz Pehlevan. I am fortunate to be supported by the Kempner Fellowship.

My main interest is in deep learning theory, and I'm currently working on in-context learning.
